1. DELIVERY STRUCTURE
- Five language directories: en, zh, ru, ar, es.
- 17 independent static HTML pages per language; 85 localized pages total.
- Six vehicle model sourcing guides in each language (Tucson, Sportage, CS55 Plus, Haval Jolion, Jetour X70, Camry).
- Kazakhstan has a dedicated market guide in all five languages.
- Each language has a global Markets hub; Kazakhstan is presented as one focus market, not as the exclusive service area.
- Each language has a used-vehicle hub, a separate auto-parts wholesale hub, four parts-category pages, service and contact pages.
- Root language redirect plus five root compatibility aliases are included (six root-level HTML files); localized pages remain in their language folders.

2. BRAND / CONTENT POSITIONING
- Global homepage positions Lianzon Auto as a China-based exporter serving international buyers.
- Two equal commercial lines: whole-vehicle export and wholesale automotive spare-parts procurement.
- Kazakhstan is accessible from the Markets hub and remains a dedicated focus market, but is not shown as the only destination.
- International coverage explicitly names Central Asia, Latin America, Africa, Europe and the Middle East.
- No empty Latin America country pages were generated merely to target keywords; this avoids thin/doorway-style landing pages.
- Vehicle model pages are sourcing/specification guides, not representations of live inventory or guaranteed prices.
