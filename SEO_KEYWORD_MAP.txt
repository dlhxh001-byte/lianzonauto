LIANZON AUTO V8 — PAGE-LEVEL SEO KEYWORD MAP
Last updated: 2026-11-10

PURPOSE
The two business lines have separate search-intent clusters and landing pages:
1) New and used whole vehicles from China (equal core business line).
2) Wholesale automotive spare parts from China (equal core business line).
The Kazakhstan page is a destination-market cluster inside the whole-vehicle topic. Model guides and parts-category pages are narrower long-tail clusters.

IMPORTANT
These are page-targeting phrases, not measured search-volume claims. They should be used naturally in page titles, H1/H2 headings, descriptions, body copy, image alt text and internal anchor text. Do not repeat exact-match phrases unnaturally. Search volume, difficulty and conversion data must be validated later in Google Search Console / keyword research tools after launch. Google does not use the meta-keywords tag for web ranking; the site therefore relies on visible semantic content and page-level metadata, not a meta-keywords tag.

INTERNAL LINKING RULES
- Homepage and top navigation give whole-vehicle pages the dominant prominence.
- The Kazakhstan destination page is linked from the homepage and vehicle hub, and links back to relevant vehicle guides and the quote page.
- Each model guide links to the vehicle hub and a pre-filled vehicle inquiry path.
- The spare-parts hub links to its four category pages; each category page links back to the parts hub and a pre-filled parts inquiry path.
- Shared contact/service pages are legitimate utility pages, not keyword substitutes for either business line.
- Language switches preserve the same page slug in the target language directory.

PUBLISHING / MEASUREMENT
- Submit /sitemap.xml in Google Search Console after deployment.
- Verify that canonical URLs return HTTP 200 and are not blocked by robots/noindex.
- Inspect the 5 language directories separately; verify hreflang reciprocity in Google's URL Inspection where available.
- Review Search Console query/page reports after sufficient crawl and impression data accumulates; refine titles and content from actual search queries.
- Rankings and indexing are not guaranteed by metadata or sitemap submission alone.
