Fitment before price

The same vehicle name can cover multiple generations and trim levels. Confirm VIN, model year, engine, chassis code and OE reference before selecting a part. For electrical and body components, connector type, side, facelift and sensor positions can be decisive.

Part condition and quality must be specified

State whether you require new, used, remanufactured, genuine-brand, OEM-supplier or aftermarket products. These terms are not interchangeable. The seller, brand, grade, warranty, packaging and any test evidence should be recorded in the written quotation.

Wholesale order and export preparation

For repeat orders, provide a spreadsheet with part number, OE reference, description, quantity and vehicle application. Mixed-SKU consolidation, carton marks, palletization, container loading and export documents can be discussed after the item list and shipment destination are confirmed.

Typical parts requested

  • Engine assemblies and cylinder-head components
  • Automatic and manual transmissions / gearboxes
  • Clutch kits, torque converters and drivetrain parts
  • Fuel, cooling and lubrication-related engine components

Frequently asked questions

Can you confirm fitment from a photo?

A photo helps identify appearance but is not enough on its own. Provide VIN, OE reference, engine or chassis code and exact vehicle application.

Are all parts genuine or OEM?

No blanket claim is made. State the brand and quality tier you require; product origin, condition, warranty and evidence must be confirmed in the written quotation.

Fitment information required

  • Engine code and vehicle VIN
  • Transmission code, drivetrain and model year
  • OE / OEM reference or original part number
  • Required condition and quality grade (new / used / remanufactured, where available)
  • Quantity, packaging and destination
