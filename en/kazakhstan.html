Choose the correct import route

The process for a private individual importing a vehicle for personal use is not the same as a dealer or company importing vehicles for resale. Confirm the importer of record, intended use and customs procedure before committing to a vehicle. We do not treat a private-use route as a substitute for commercial import clearance.

Match the China specification to the Kazakhstan requirement

A vehicle name alone is not enough. Different markets may use different model codes, engines, emissions equipment, safety equipment, steering configurations and trim levels. We compare the exact Chinese-market vehicle with the specification requested by the Kazakhstan buyer, using the VIN and manufacturer or registration documents wherever available.

International logistics and delivery arrangements

A quotation can set out vehicle sourcing, condition evidence, export documentation, and international transport and delivery arrangements. Routes from China to Kazakhstan are assessed according to the vehicle, destination and customer requirements. If Khorgos or another delivery point is requested, the written quotation should specify the destination, transport method, loading, insurance and each party’s customs responsibilities. Kazakhstan import duties, taxes, certification and registration must be checked separately unless expressly included in the quotation.

Documents and identity checks

Before shipment, compare the VIN or chassis number, model year, engine details, seller and buyer names, invoice or contract, ownership evidence and transport documents. Kazakhstan authorities list identity documents, vehicle documents, a contract or invoice and transport paperwork among the documents relevant to individual vehicle import procedures. Requirements vary by import route.

Kazakhstan customs, certification and registration

Kazakhstan customs guidance distinguishes personal-use imports from commercial imports. Additional steps may include conformity or safety documentation, electronic vehicle passport (EPTS), recycling-fee confirmation and initial registration. The applicable sequence and cost depend on the importer, vehicle and current law. Verify the current rules with the State Revenue Committee and a licensed customs representative before purchase.

What to include in your inquiry

Send the target make and model, model year range, engine and trim, left- or right-hand drive requirement, quantity, budget range, destination city and whether the importer is an individual or a business. For a specific vehicle, request VIN-linked photographs, mileage evidence, condition notes and document samples before payment.

Important: do not pay a deposit until the importer type, vehicle specification, export eligibility, destination-side import route and delivery responsibilities are clear in writing.
Official Kazakhstan references (verify current rules before purchase):
  • Department of State Revenues — Customs clearance of motor vehicles imported by individuals (8 Sep 2026)
  • Department of State Revenues — Amendments to customs clearance requirements (29 Sep 2026)
  • State Revenue Committee — Procedure for importing goods for personal use (5 Nov 2026)

This information is a practical checklist, not legal or customs advice. Rules, rates and required documents can change. Confirm the exact case with Kazakhstan authorities or a licensed customs representative.

Frequently asked questions

Can you arrange transport to Khorgos or another destination?

Where the vehicle and export route are eligible, we can assess international transport and delivery from China to Khorgos or another destination agreed by both parties. The destination, transport method, loading arrangements and each party’s responsibilities should be stated in the written quotation.

Does a private buyer use the same customs route as a car dealer?

No. Kazakhstan guidance distinguishes vehicles imported by individuals for personal use from commercial imports. The importer and intended use must be established before choosing the route.

Are Kazakhstan taxes and registration included?

Not by default. Customs charges, recycling fees, certification and registration should be separately confirmed for the specific vehicle and importer.

Can a China-market Tucson or Sportage be assumed identical to a Kazakhstan-market version?

No. Model names can cover different generations, powertrains and equipment. Confirm the exact model code, VIN, engine and specification before purchase.
